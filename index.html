The visit matters for permitted development too

Permitted development is not automatic. Whether your project qualifies depends on facts that can only be confirmed at the house, so I visit before drawing anything, including for a Lawful Development Certificate.

  • Limits are measured from the original house. Earlier extensions and roof additions use up your allowance.
  • Height limits tighten near a boundary. They are measured from the ground level beside the building, so slopes matter.
  • Some homes have restricted rights. A planning condition, an Article 4 direction or a conservation area can limit what is allowed.
  • A drawing that is slightly out is not lawful. Getting the measurements right on site means the council can confirm it first time.

Questions people ask

Is the home visit really free?

Yes. It is 30 minutes at your home with me, and there is no obligation to go ahead afterwards.

What’s in the home visit report?

It’s written by me after I’ve been to your house, with photos from the visit. It covers what I found on site (boundaries, levels, drains and the neighbours’ windows), whether permitted development or a planning application is the right route, a rough cost range and the next steps. It comes with your fixed-price quote and it’s yours to keep.

Do I still need a visit if my project is permitted development?

Yes. Permitted development limits are measured from the original house, the boundary and the ground levels, and earlier extensions use up your allowance. Those can only be confirmed on site.

Do I need planning permission?

It depends on the house and the design. Many single-storey rear extensions, loft conversions and garage conversions can be done under permitted development. I will tell you which route applies at the visit.

How much do the drawings cost?

You get one fixed price after the visit, before any work starts. It includes unlimited revisions up to planning submission.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit when you instruct me, and the balance when your first draft is ready, charged to the same card before the drawings are released. Or pay in full upfront if you prefer. Pay online in seconds by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.

Can you help me find a builder?

Yes. I can introduce you to suitable builders from my network of local contractors.
