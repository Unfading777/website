The free home visit

Thirty minutes at your house tells me more than any form.

You show me what you want to do, and I look at the things that decide what's possible and what it will cost. You get straight answers on the day, then a fixed price for the drawings.

BOUNDARIES

How close you can build

Distances to each boundary, which set the height and size limits for the extension.

LEVELS

Steps and slopes

Changes in level between the house and garden that affect the design and the build cost.

DRAINS

Where the drains run

Manholes and pipe runs under the new work, which can mean a build-over agreement.

NEIGHBOURS

Windows next door

The 45° line from the neighbours' nearest windows, which planners will check.

THE ORIGINAL HOUSE

Your permitted development allowance

Earlier extensions use up your allowance, so I work out what the original house was.

Why I visit, even for permitted development

Permitted development isn't automatic. Whether your project qualifies depends on measurements that can only be checked at the house. Getting them right on site means the council can confirm it first time.

Questions

Before you book.

Is the home visit really free?

Yes. Thirty minutes, no charge and no obligation. If you'd like to go ahead, I send a fixed price for the drawings afterwards.

Do I need planning permission?

Many single-storey rear extensions and loft conversions fall under permitted development. Larger or two-storey schemes usually need a householder application. I can tell you which route applies once I've seen the house.

Why do you need to visit for permitted development?

The limits are measured from the original house and the ground beside it, and earlier extensions count towards them. Those facts can only be confirmed on site.

Which areas do you cover?

Maidenhead, Reading and the towns between, including Windsor, Marlow, Henley, Twyford and Wokingham. Send me your postcode if you're not sure.

Can you help me find a builder?

Yes. When your drawings are ready, I can introduce you to local builders I know.
