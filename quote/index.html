What is included

    What happens after you sign

    1. You pay your deposit securely online, and we agree a date for me to come back to your home.
    2. I return in person to carry out a full measured survey of your home, and we go through your brief together in detail. I then write it up for you, so we both agree exactly what is being designed before I start.
    3. I prepare your first draft within one week of the survey, with the design modelled in 3D.
    4. We revise it together until you are happy, on site, by phone or by email.
    5. I submit the application and deal with the council for you.
    6. After approval, I prepare the Building Regulations drawings and introduce you to local builders.
